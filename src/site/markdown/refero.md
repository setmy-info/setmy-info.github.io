# Refero

## Information

`Refero` (refero.design) is a massive library of design references and patterns curated from the world's best-designed
software products. It allows teams to see how leading companies solve specific UI and UX challenges in their live
applications.

In practice, teams use `Refero` to research real-world implementation of components, user flows, and design systems. It
is an invaluable resource for competitive analysis and for finding proven solutions to common interface problems.

It is especially relevant for designers and product managers who want to see how "best-in-class" products handle
everything from complex dashboards to simple onboarding flows.

### Main Functionalities and Features

* **Real-World Component Search**: Search for specific components (e.g., "pricing tables", "empty states") across
  hundreds of top products.
* **Product-Specific Galleries**: Explore the entire design language and user flows of individual successful apps.
* **UX Pattern Discovery**: Identify common and effective UX patterns used by industry leaders.
* **Design System Insights**: Gain visibility into how major companies structure their interfaces and branding.

### How Refero Fits Into Design Work

`Refero` is a reference and research platform. It is not a component library or a design tool itself.

It is often used when:

* a team is designing a new feature and wants to see how industry leaders have implemented it,
* designers need to justify UI decisions with real-world examples,
* or product teams are conducting competitive research on user experience and interface patterns.

### Common Use Cases

* Research how different products handle complex data visualization or navigation.
* Gather examples of effective empty states, error messages, or success notifications.
* Analyze the onboarding flows of successful SaaS products.
* Create "best practice" benchmarks for new interface developments.

### Practical Notes

* `Refero` provides snapshots of live products; remember that design trends and product features evolve, so check for
  the most current versions when possible.
* While it's great for inspiration and benchmarking, always adapt patterns to your specific user needs and brand
  identity rather than copying blindly.
